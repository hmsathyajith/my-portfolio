01 — About

More than the stack trace

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Vital Hub Innovations Lab, where I design microservices for real-time healthcare visibility platforms used across the UK's NHS ecosystem. My days are split between untangling event-driven architectures with RabbitMQ, shaping clean APIs in Spring Boot, and building interfaces that don't feel like enterprise software in Angular.

I got here from a Computer Science degree at the University of Kelaniya, a stint at Virtusa shadowing seniors on Java EE systems, and a genuine habit of asking "why does this break in production" until I actually know the answer. Outside of sprint ceremonies, I mentor junior engineers, run knowledge-transfer sessions, and lead the kind of code reviews that catch things before they become 2am pages.

When I'm not shipping features, I'm usually at the gym staying in shape, chasing new places to eat, or sketching — the discipline carries over from my competitive karate days (former university team captain). I still introduce myself as "programmer + art" before anything else.
